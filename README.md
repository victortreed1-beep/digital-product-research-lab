# Digital Product Research Lab

A personal Windows application that researches buyer pain points to identify small, original digital products, including spreadsheets, templates, printables, guides, and simple tools.

## Intended workflow

Collect authorized review evidence, preserve source links, identify recurring complaints, and generate a narrowly scoped product brief with essential features, tentative build effort, and a price hypothesis.

## Current status

Business Opportunity Finder v10 generates reports. Scrapling HTTP fetching is tested. The digital-product adapter has passed fixture tests, but live Etsy review collection and local AI integration have not been verified or activated.

Etsy data collection and automated analysis will remain pending express written authorization from Etsy. TikTok comment access is not connected. No AI model training is planned.

## Access and data

The application is intended for the owner's personal use. Credentials remain local and are not published. It does not automatically modify listings, access sales records, send messages, or resell platform data.

## Research limitations

Product briefs are investigation hypotheses. They do not establish validated demand, sales, profitability, or guaranteed build time.
